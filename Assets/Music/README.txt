BLUROOM BATTLEFIELD — Music Files
==================================

Drop your audio files here. Supported formats: .ogg (recommended) or .mp3

  menu.ogg    — plays on the Home screen (loops)
  select.ogg  — plays on the Character Select screen (loops)
  battle.ogg  — plays during battles (loops)

.ogg is preferred over .mp3 because pygame handles it more reliably
across all platforms without extra codecs.

Free music resources:
  https://opengameart.org
  https://freemusicarchive.org
  https://incompetech.com
